# University identity assets

Fifty-two institution marks sourced from the UniLogo / ARWU collection, pinned to commit d64657c0f29817d8beaaf541104446c54d39c383. No logos were generated or redrawn. The images retain the original source bytes. Each university retains rights to its marks; these identify schools in the catalogue and do not imply endorsement.

The Villanova University seal is retained from a pinned Villanova-Project mirror (kianeutron/Villanova-Project); its original canvas is displayed with CSS object-fit: cover to avoid excessive horizontal whitespace. No source image bytes are modified.

All 53 catalogue institutions now have identity images, including the 51 schools in the supplied ranking PDF and two retained legacy records. See manifest.json for exact source URLs and checksums.
