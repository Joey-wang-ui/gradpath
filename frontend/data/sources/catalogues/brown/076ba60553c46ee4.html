Master's Programs at Brown

With more than 30 master’s degree programs, you have the opportunity to study and research a diverse range of topics that align with your personal and professional goals. Through a variety of teaching modalities — including on campus, online and hybrid options — and flexible degree timelines starting at just one year, Brown’s master’s programs offer an innovative, dynamic and versatile learning experience.

Brown undergraduates, please visit the Fifth-Year Master's page for a comprehensive list of programs.
