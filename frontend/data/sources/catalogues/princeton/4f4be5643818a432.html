Fields of Study

The Graduate School at Princeton University awards more than 600 advanced degrees annually across 47 departments and programs. Our degree programs are housed within four divisions: humanities, natural sciences, social sciences, and engineering. As part of the graduate experience, the University also offers certificates, interdepartmental programs, and established joint-degree offerings that cross disciplinary boundaries. Learn more about our program offerings.
