Explore graduate programs using the search functions below, allowing you to browse by area of interest, degree type or academic school. 

At Northwestern University, PhD and MFA programs—including dual degrees and select master’s degrees (such as the MA and MPH)—are administered centrally through The Graduate School (TGS). Professional master’s and doctoral programs (including the MBA, MS, MA, MPO, MD, JD, SLPD, and AuD) are administered by the Northwestern school that confers the degree.

Follow the program links below for comprehensive information, including curriculum details and admissions requirements. For graduate certificate programs or continuing education offerings visit our Continuing and Professional Programs page.
