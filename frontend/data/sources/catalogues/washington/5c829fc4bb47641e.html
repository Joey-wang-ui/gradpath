More than 15,000 University of Washington graduate students are learning, discovering and innovating in fields as diverse as drama and dentistry, philosophy and public policy, English and engineering. With more than 300 graduate programs across our three campuses, the UW offers many paths to a graduate education.

Learn more about our graduate degree programs and graduate certificates to find the one that’s right for you.
