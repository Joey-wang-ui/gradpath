Graduate Programs

 

A transformative graduate education

Graduate education is integral to the University of Chicago’s mission. Today, more than 100 graduate programs are available at UChicago, offering students a transformative educational experience while preparing them to make an impact in their careers—in areas ranging from academia to industry to government and nonprofits.

Learn more about the diverse degree programs at UChicago, which offer students flexibility through full-time, part-time, executive, and online formats:

WATCH: A UChicago Master’s Education

At the University of Chicago, we believe the boldest leaders are those who never stop questioning. Our master’s programs, spanning fields from finance to the digital humanities, are aimed at students who are “endlessly curious”—eager to learn from world-renowned faculty and help shape our world.
